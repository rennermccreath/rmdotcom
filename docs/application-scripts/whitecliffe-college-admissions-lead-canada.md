# Whitecliffe College | Admissions Lead – Canada

## Introduction (45–60 seconds)

Hi, I am Renner McCreath, and I am applying for the Admissions Lead – Canada role with Whitecliffe College. In my current role with Boreal Education, I provide enrolment guidance to undergraduate and postgraduate students and remain a primary point of contact throughout their learning journey. I own the process from lead to enrolment and tailor engagement plans to individual student needs.

Before Boreal, I worked in customer success at Fruit Suite, where I led trial conversion and new-client onboarding while helping the company reach its strongest financial year. Earlier in my career at CI Global Asset Management, I supported sales enablement, stakeholder communication, and complex operational projects across Canadian financial institutions. The common thread is helping people make informed decisions, then building the structure needed to deliver on them. I would welcome the opportunity to bring that approach to Whitecliffe's first Canadian cohort.

## Why Whitecliffe and why this role (60–90 seconds)

What interests me about Whitecliffe is the opportunity to help build something meaningful in Canada from the ground up. The Toronto campus is preparing to welcome its first cohort for the Master of Creative Art Therapy, and the Admissions Lead role combines thoughtful guidance with the practical work of building a reliable admissions function.

That combination fits how I work. At Boreal, I support students across undergraduate and postgraduate programs, provide enrolment advice, and create engagement plans around individual needs. I understand that choosing a program is a significant decision, so the goal is not simply to move someone through a funnel. It is to listen carefully, explain the available path clearly, and help a prospective student decide whether the program is genuinely right for them.

I also bring experience creating structure around growth. At Fruit Suite, I helped improve trial conversion and onboarding practices in a small, hands-on team. At CI Global Asset Management, I managed concurrent sales-enablement projects, coordinated stakeholders, and supported new product approvals and promotion. I would bring that same combination of empathy, pipeline ownership, careful follow-through, and clear communication to Whitecliffe as it develops its Ontario admissions pipeline.
